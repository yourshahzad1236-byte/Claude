1. Introduction

The HOPe PA Performance Survey is the online form used for annual performance appraisals. One survey is opened per employee per review period. The employee (self-appraisal) and each appraiser in the hierarchy complete their parts, then the appraisal is forwarded to the Human Resources (HR) department.

The page title shows the appraisal reference, for example PA Performance <template code> | <employee MR/ID number> | <employee name>. Always check this header to confirm you are working on the correct person's appraisal.

2. Opening the survey

  1. Log in to the HMIS with your employee ID and password.
  2. From the main menu choose Human Resources and open the Performance Appraisal entry.
  3. Select the appraisal from your list. The PA Performance page opens.
  4. Use the Refresh icon (top right of the PA Performance region) to reload data at any time.
Tip: Your name, department, terminal and login time appear in the footer. Click your name to open the profile window (Edit Profile, Change Password, Logout).

5. Behavioral Assessment

  1. Click Edit in the grid toolbar.
  2. For each Parameter (with its Description), pick a rating from the Rating list. Use the Rating Scale panel beside the grid to see what each value means.
  3. Previous-year ratings (e.g. 2025, 2024) are shown beside the current year for comparison.
  4. Click Save.

The summary panel updates automatically:

FieldMeaning
Total ScoreMaximum possible score for all parameters.
Obtained ScoreSum of ratings given.
Marks After DeductionObtained score after any mark deduction (see below).
Performance %Marks after deduction ÷ total score.
GradeDerived from Performance % (see Grading scale).
FAR (Behavioural Assessment)Final Aggregate Rating = Performance × 0.3 (30% weightage).

Mark Deduction: where applicable, a Mark Deduction button appears to apply a deduction. A previous-year deduction, if any, is shown as Previous Year Deduction.

Note: If Performance % shows NaN or the grid says “No data found”, no rating parameters have been loaded or saved yet. Save your ratings or press Refresh.

6. Management Traits

Under “From the below list of adjectives, choose the 4 that best describe you”, tick the checkbox beside exactly four adjectives and click Save. Selecting more or fewer than four will not be accepted.

7. Discussion Points

This tab lists numbered questions, for example:

  1. How would you describe your professional experience over the past year?
  2. What elements of your job do you find most difficult?
  3. What elements of your job interest you the most?
  4. What do you consider to be your most important aims and tasks in the coming year?
  5. What action(s) could you take to improve your performance in your current position?
  6. What action(s) could your supervisor take to improve your performance?

Steps:

  1. Click Edit.
  2. Type your answer in the current-year column. Use the editor button to open a larger text window; click OK to return.
  3. Earlier years' answers appear in the adjacent columns (read-only) for reference.
  4. Click Save.
Tip: Use Single-row view to read and write long answers comfortably, and Previous/Next to move between questions.

8. Peer Nomination

The Peers to be Nominated grid lists staff categories and how many peers are required from each, e.g.:

CategoryRequired peers
Pharmacist / Technician / Technologist2
Consultant / Senior Instructors / Ancillary Health Practitioners6
Nurses4
Health Care Assistant / Secretary2

(Numbers vary by template.)

  1. Select a category row. The matching “List of Employees to Choose Peers From” grid opens below (internal staff), or an external-peer grid (name, contact details, email) for external nominations.
  2. Click Edit, then Add Row.
  3. Choose the peer from the Name pop-up list; Employee Code, Designation and Department fill in automatically. For external peers, type name, contact details and email.
  4. Repeat until you reach the required number, then click Save.
  5. To remove a peer, use the Delete control on that row and Save.
  6. If a category does not apply, tick Not Applicable for that row.
Note: Nominate each person only once and stay within the required count. Use Refresh after saving to confirm the totals.

9. Medical Education / Research

Shown only for eligible medical staff. Open the tab, click Edit, complete the education/research entries and Save.

10. Finalization

The Finalization area shows Appraisers' Remarks and a checklist: “Complete the item(s) below before forwarding to HR” (for example Discussion Points, Peer Nomination, Medical Education/Research).

  1. Complete every tab listed in the checklist.
  2. Enter your comments under Add your Remarks (Appraiser Remarks).
  3. Choose an action:
    • To Previous Appraiser – returns the form for correction.
    • Forward Appraisal to HR Department – sends the completed appraisal on.
  4. Use Preview to review the full form, Save to keep progress, and Exit to leave.
Important: Review all tabs before forwarding. Once forwarded, you can no longer edit the appraisal.

12. Troubleshooting & FAQ

ProblemSolution
I cannot type in a grid.Press Edit to switch to edit mode.
My entries disappeared.They were not saved. Always click Save before changing tab or leaving; Reset also discards edits.
A tab is missing.Tabs depend on your role and template. Contact HR if you believe you need access.
“No data found” in a grid.Nothing has been assigned or saved yet. Press Refresh; if it persists contact HR.
Performance % shows NaN.Ratings have not been saved. Enter and save ratings, then refresh.
I cannot find a peer.Search in the Name list by name or code. If the person is external, use the external-peer grid.
I forwarded by mistake.Contact HR to have the appraisal returned.
Session timed out.Log in again and reopen the appraisal; saved data is retained.

Support: HR Performance Management / IT Helpdesk. Keep appraisal content confidential.
